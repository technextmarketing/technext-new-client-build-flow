New client info in, full client pack out

When new client information arrives, it becomes one client profile. Five outputs are built from that profile at the same time, starting with the sales proposal. They pass the checks, you review them once, and the client gets one consistent pack.

Step 4 · Checks, then your review

Nothing leaves before it passes and you approve

Automatic checks, before you see anything

  • Sales proposal: Impeccable critique and audit, hero check 0 errors, prices only in the commercial part
  • Data page: opens on a phone, every field saves, no other client's data
  • Presentation: Impeccable critique and audit, issues fixed and re-scored
  • SLA: every page fits above the footer, figures tagged Estimate
  • Posts and video: audit.py 0 errors, Nexi release gate passed

Copy rules on all five

  • No BIR guarantees and no "BIR-ready" or accreditation claims; the client's accountant confirms coverage
  • Odoo is never "free": the client pays the Odoo subscription
  • Odoo Partner in Singapore only
  • One client per pack: no other client's name, logo or link
You

One review link shows the five outputs side by side. You approve, or say what to change and only that part is rebuilt.

Nothing reaches the client or social media before you approve.

When each part goes out

  1. First reply to the clientSales Proposal link and the Short Presentation, in a Gmail draft from hello@technext.asia. You press send.
  2. When they accept the proposalThe Data Gathering link goes out so they can send every detail we need.
  3. After their answers come inThe SLA is rebuilt with the real details as the next revision and sent for signature.
  4. After signing and their OK on name and logoWelcome posts and the Nexi video go into the posting schedule.

To decide before we build the automation

  • Where the Data Gathering page saves answers and files: the client's Odoo CRM record, or their folder on the Marketing drive.
  • Whether to add a publicity line to the SLA, so the client agrees up front to the welcome post and Nexi video.
  • How each client's page is hosted: a private link per client, with no TechNext site menu and no other client's links.
