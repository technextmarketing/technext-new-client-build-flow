NextCore

New client info in, full client pack out.

NextCore takes a new client through six stages, from the first notes to kick-off. Each stage builds what it needs from one client profile, waits at the decisions only the client can make, and sends nothing until you approve.

To decide before we build NextCore

  • How NextCore learns the client accepted or signed: you tell it, or the proposal page reports it.
  • Where the Data Gathering page saves answers and files: the client's Odoo CRM record, or their folder on the Marketing drive.
  • Where each client's proposal and data page are hosted: a private link per client, with no TechNext site menu and no other client's links.
  • Whether to add a publicity line to the SLA, so the client agrees to the welcome post and Nexi video when they sign.
  • Follow-up timing: day 3, day 7 and day 14 is a first proposal.
